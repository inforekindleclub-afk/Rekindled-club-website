the founder

Hi, I'm Zainab.

I'm 24, a co-founder of a creative agency in Sri Lanka, and I've spent the last while documenting every part of building something real — the messy, uncertain, beautiful version of it.

I know what it feels like to have a vision that doesn't fit the norm. To be in a community where the path is already laid out, and to quietly wonder — is there another way for me? Not because I don't love where I come from. I do, deeply. But I wanted to build a space for women who feel the same quiet question — a place to figure it out together instead of alone.

That's Rekindled Club. Not a business first. A sisterhood first.

how we work

A few things we hold onto

1

Sister energy, not guru energy

No advice from a pedestal, ever. This is a space we build side by side.

2

Curated, not open

Every member is chosen, not signed up. It keeps this feeling like something real.

3

Community first, always

If anything ever starts to feel like it's optimizing for growth over belonging, we stop and re-check it.

"I'm not here to rebel, or to fight anyone. I'm here to come out of the bubble, with my faith intact."

— Zainab, Founder · @documentwith.zaiinab
