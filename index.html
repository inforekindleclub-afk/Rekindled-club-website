what this is

A community first. A club second.

Rekindled Club is a curated, sister-led personal development community for women across Sri Lanka — built by Zainab. Not a course platform, not content to scroll past — an actual space, small enough that everyone in it is known.

the four pillars

Everything lives inside one of these

01

Personality Development

Reading, journaling, communication, confidence, habits — lean into whichever fits you. Nobody has to be "a reader" to belong.

02

Business & Financial Guidance

Idea validation, managing money, knowing your pros and cons before you start — real guidance, not theory.

03

Wellbeing & Self-Care

Rest, boundaries, managing overwhelm — held gently, without needing to explain yourself.

04

Career & Skills Growth

For the woman building a career, not just a business — skills, confidence, and direction.

how it starts

Small, on purpose

1

The First Spark

A live welcome session — where you actually meet Zainab and hear what this is, before anything else.

2

The founding circle

A small, curated group — chosen, not open sign-up. This is where the club actually begins.

3

Together, weekly

Online sessions every week, in-person meetups when we can, and a community that talks back — not just listens.
