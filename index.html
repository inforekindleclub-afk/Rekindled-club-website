about

Not becoming someone new. Coming back to who you were.

Rekindled Club is a curated, sister-led personal development community for women across Sri Lanka — an actual space, small enough that everyone in it is known, not content to scroll past.

Vision

A Sri Lanka where a woman doesn't have to choose between the life she was handed and the one she actually wants — where ambition, faith, and sisterhood are allowed to grow together instead of pulling her in different directions.

Mission

Rekindled Club gives women across Sri Lanka a small, curated, sister-led space to grow — personally, financially, in their careers, and in how they care for themselves — together, weekly, with real people instead of content to scroll past.

meet the founder

Hi, I'm Zainab.

main photo
coming soon
photo 2
photo 3

I'm 24, a co-founder of a creative agency in Sri Lanka, and I've spent the last while documenting every part of building something real — the messy, uncertain, beautiful version of it.

I know what it feels like to have a vision that doesn't fit the norm. To be in a community where the path is already laid out, and to quietly wonder — is there another way for me? Not because I don't love where I come from. I do, deeply. But I wanted to build a space for women who feel the same quiet question.

That's Rekindled Club. Not a business first. A sisterhood first.

"This is for the girl who desperately wants a different life — not because her life is bad, but because she knows there's more inside her than what's been decided for her."

— Zainab, Founder · @documentwith.zaiinab
